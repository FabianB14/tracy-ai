## The idea

Junkyards hold inventory nobody can search. PartOut gives a yard a dead simple way
to list pulls: photo in, AI fills in the part number and fitment. PartOut becomes
the search layer for junkyards, monetized as revenue share or a monthly SaaS fee
per yard. The existing Pull Guide feature already tells people HOW to remove a
part. This tells them WHERE the part is. Builds on [[e-product-partout]].

## Why it matters to Interverse

Supply is the constraint on any young parts marketplace. Yard inventory is bulk
supply that arrives through one business deal instead of one seller at a time,
and a per-yard SaaS fee is recurring B2B revenue on top of marketplace take.
If the listing flow really is photo-to-fitment, PartOut's AI scanning is the
differentiator incumbents lack.

## Open questions

- Official count of licensed motor vehicle wreckers in WA (DOL publishes no

- Will self-service and small independent yards actually photo-list pulls, and

- Why did Row52 shrink to Pick-n-Pull only? Business failure or market signal?
