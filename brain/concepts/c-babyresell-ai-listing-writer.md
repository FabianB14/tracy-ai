## The idea

Sellers photograph an item and the app writes the listing: title, description,
condition notes, suggested price from comparable sold listings. Same pattern as
PartOut's AI Mechanic, applied to listing creation on [[e-product-babyresell]].

## Why it matters to Interverse

Listing friction is the top reason casual sellers abandon two-sided marketplaces.
Cutting listing time from minutes to seconds grows supply, and supply is the
constraint on a young marketplace. Also a differentiator against Facebook
Marketplace, which offers nothing like it.

## Open questions

- What do Mercari, Poshmark, and eBay currently offer for AI-assisted listings,
  and how good are they?
- Cheapest viable model for image-to-listing at BabyResell's scale?
- Does suggested pricing need sold-comp data BabyResell does not have yet?
