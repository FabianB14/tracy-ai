## Summary

Interverse Corp is Fabian Brooks's company (Fabian: founder and CEO; Josh: CTO).
It ships an ecosystem of consumer products plus a game platform, tied together
by one AI assistant.

The four pillars:

1. **INTERVERSE platform**: a cross-game asset platform. An item minted in one
   game is owned by the player and honored (recognized and usable) in other
   games. Runs on a custom Proof of Stake chain ("VERSE") with the IVX token,
   fixed 100M supply. Studios integrate via the Interverse SDK: Unreal 5.5+
   (most complete, Blueprint support), Unity 2022+, RPG Maker, and Python
   (pip install interverse-sdk). Live API at interverse-api.onrender.com.
   Tiers: Starter, Pro, Enterprise.
2. **[[e-product-babyresell]]**: the live flagship, a two-sided resale
   marketplace for baby and kids items, and the architectural template for
   vertical marketplaces.
3. **[[e-product-partout]]**: the car app. AI part scanning, valuation, an AI
   Mechanic, pull guides, and salvage part-out tracking.
4. **[[e-product-tracy]]**: the AI assistant layer. One brain, many surfaces,
   embedded across the ecosystem and consuming this knowledge base.

## Key facts

- BabyResell is live in production with real payments and shipping. [first-party]
- The platform's cross-game mechanism is honoring, not conversion; the old
  convert-asset path is deprecated. [first-party]
- Developer onboarding runs through Discord (#developer-onboarding) with
  Game ID, API Key, and license keys per tier. [first-party]

## The play

The repeatable motion: pick a vertical with trapped supply, ship a focused
marketplace or tool on the BabyResell template, give it intelligence by adding
a Tracy surface instead of building a new AI, and let the platform side grow
through SDK integrations. Evaluate every new idea against reuse of these four
pillars before green-lighting new infrastructure.
